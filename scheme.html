Scheme types

How irrigation schemes are generally classified.

Public schemes

Developed and managed by government agencies, usually large and serving many farmers through shared canals and pumps.

Smallholder schemes

Community-run schemes where farmers share water from rivers, springs or dams and manage the system collectively.

Private schemes

Commercial farms and agribusinesses investing in their own irrigation infrastructure, sometimes in partnership with government.
